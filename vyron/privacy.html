Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VYRON handles information when a user connects Google and YouTube accounts to the VYRON macOS desktop application.

Information VYRON accesses

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, VYRON may access YouTube channel information, video metadata, playlists, publication status, and YouTube Analytics data. Where the user explicitly grants monetary analytics permission, VYRON may also display revenue-related YouTube Analytics metrics returned by Google.

How the information is used

  • To identify the YouTube channel connected by the user.
  • To display channel, video and analytics information inside VYRON.
  • To allow the user to manage video metadata and publishing settings.
  • To support channel-specific automation initiated or configured by the user.

Storage and security

VYRON is a desktop application. Google OAuth tokens and VYRON application state are stored locally on the user's device for the purpose of maintaining authorized access. VYRON does not intentionally publish OAuth tokens or Google credentials and does not sell Google user data.

Sharing

VYRON does not sell, rent or trade Google user data. Data is used to provide the functionality requested by the user. Google/YouTube API requests are made to Google services as required for those features.

Google API Services User Data Policy

VYRON's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

User control and revocation

Users can disconnect a YouTube account from VYRON and can revoke VYRON's Google account access from their Google Account permissions. Local application data can also be removed from the user's device.

Data retention

Locally stored OAuth and application data is retained while the user keeps the account connected or the local VYRON application data remains on the device. Revoking access prevents VYRON from obtaining new Google/YouTube data with the revoked authorization.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to vryranaut@gmail.com.
